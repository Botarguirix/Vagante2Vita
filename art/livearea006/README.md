## Prompt del icono

Create an original production-ready PS Vita LiveArea launcher ICON, square 1:1. Dark fantasy pixel-art aesthetic appropriate for Vagante2Vita. A single gold-bronze capital V shaped from a sword and intertwined woodland branch, centered on a deep midnight teal background, warm amber highlights, subtle moon halo. Strong readable silhouette at 128x128 pixels, thick distinct edges, sparse detailed texture. Keep all important artwork inside the center 70% of square so a circular bubble crop does not cut it. No border outside safe circle, no other words, no console logos, no watermark. Original artwork rather than reproduce a game screenshot. Opaque background.

## Prompt del fondo

Create an original landscape 1.68:1 aspect-ratio background illustration for the PS Vita LiveArea of Vagante2Vita. Rich pixel-art dark fantasy woodland at night: layered ancient forest, distant mountains, a winding path disappearing toward a cave, a small warmly lit wooden caravan in the lower left, pale moon and a few fireflies, dark navy and muted forest green with bronze amber illumination. Crisp pixelated edges and atmospheric depth, no blur or photorealism. Composition keeps the central and right portions calm and dark for the system launch panel. Place only the exact title 'VAGANTE2VITA' in legible restrained gold pixel lettering in the upper left, with generous margins, no other text. This is original project artwork, not an official game cover or screenshot. No UI frames or buttons, no watermark. Fill the entire opaque canvas.
